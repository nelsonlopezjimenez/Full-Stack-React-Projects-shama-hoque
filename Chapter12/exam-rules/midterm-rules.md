# Midterm — Rules and Procedure

A printable one-page version is in [midterm-rules.html](midterm-rules.html): open it in a browser and print it (Ctrl+P).

## Before you begin

- At the top of your paper, write **your name**, **the date**, and **"Midterm"**.
- Put away your textbook, laptop, phone and any other reference material. **Your own notes are the only thing you may use.**
- From now until the end of the midterm, **do not talk** to other students.

## How the questions are given

- **One question at a time.** The instructor shows a question, you answer it, then everyone moves on to the next one together.
- **You have 2 minutes per question.**
- **Clarifications come first.** When a question is shown, you may ask what it means *before* the timer starts. The time spent on clarifications **does not count** toward your 2 minutes. Once the timer starts, no more questions.

## When the 2 minutes are up

- **Draw a horizontal line right under your answer.** This closes the question. Do not go back to a question you've already closed.

## How to answer

- **If you have time,** write as many details as you can: steps, examples, code, the reasons why.
- **If time is short,** sum up your answer in one or two sentences.
- **If you know the answer,** state it clearly and briefly first, then add details if time allows.
- **If you are not sure,** write your best guess.
- **If you have no guess,** write something related to the topic: a term, a concept, a line of code, anything you know about it.
- **Never leave an answer blank.** A partial answer can earn points; a blank one cannot.
